Privacy Summary: Plantii respects your privacy. We do not require accounts with personal credentials, do not sell data, do not display ads, and never store leaf photos on our backend servers. Text-to-speech runs entirely on your phone.

1. Who We Are

Plantii is an agricultural technology application created and operated by Prakuty Tech, based in Chhattisgarh, India. Our mission is to provide farmers with fast, reliable, and accessible crop health diagnostics and leaf disease treatment advice. For questions or grievance redressal, contact us at info@prakruty.com.

2. What We Collect

We practice strict data minimization and collect only the data necessary to provide crop diagnostics:

  • Anonymous User ID: Firebase Anonymous Authentication assigns a random, pseudonymous identifier (UID). We do NOT collect your name, phone number, email address, or government identity.
  • Plant Leaf Photos: When you snap or select a leaf photo, it is sent securely to our backend and Google's Gemini Vision AI service for diagnostic analysis. Our backend servers do NOT store your photos.
  • Local Storage: A copy of your leaf photo is saved locally in your phone's storage so you can review your past scans in the History tab offline.
  • Diagnosis & Scan Records: We store your anonymous user ID, crop type, selected language, disease name, diagnosis text, severity rating, confidence level, treatments, and timestamp in Cloud Firestore.
  • Feedback: If you submit helpfulness ratings (thumbs up/down) or optional comments, they are stored with the scan ID.
  • Abuse Prevention & Limits: To prevent denial-of-service and fair usage, our backend records a one-way SHA-256 hashed (not raw) IP address and daily scan counts. We NEVER store raw IP addresses in Firestore.
  • Device Integrity: Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity verify genuine requests from certified Android devices.
  • Device Permissions: Camera permission is used solely to capture plant photos. Gallery permission is used solely to choose a plant photo you select.
  • Text-to-Speech: The read-aloud voice feature runs entirely on your device using the on-device text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded or transmitted to any server.

3. Why We Use It

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • To analyze plant leaf symptoms and provide organic and chemical treatments.
  • To display your personal scan history on your device.
  • To protect the service against automated bot abuse (daily quota of 10 scans per user; 30 requests per IP per hour).
  • Zero Ads & Zero Tracking: We do NOT sell your data, do NOT use advertising networks, and do NOT track you across third-party apps or websites.

4. Who We Share It With

We disclose data strictly to essential infrastructure providers:

  • Google Gemini API: The plant photo is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS through our backend to Google's Gemini Vision API solely for image analysis. The image is processed in-memory and is not stored on our servers.
  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Provides backend cloud functions (hosted in Mumbai / asia-south1 region for ultra-low latency in India), Cloud Firestore database, and App Check security.
  • We never sell or disclose your data to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

5. How Long We Keep It

Data retention is governed by automated server cleanup schedules:

  • Leaf Photos on Server: 0 seconds (processed in memory, never stored on disk).
  • Local Phone Photos: Kept on your device until you clear history or remove app data.
  • IP Rate Limit Records: Automatically purged after 2 days (48 hours) by our daily scheduled server cleanup function.
  • System Aggregated Stats: Retained for 90 days for system health and billing protection.
  • Scan Records & Feedback: Stored under your anonymous ID until you choose "Delete my data" in Settings.

6. Your Rights (Access, Deletion & Consent)

You have full authority over your data:

  • Access: Review all past diagnoses in the History tab.
  • Instant Deletion: Tap "Delete my data" in Settings to permanently delete your scans, feedback, user document, and Firebase Auth account from our servers and clear your local phone data.
  • Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings. Scanning features will remain paused until you choose to consent again.

7. Security

All communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. IP addresses are hashed using SHA-256 with a secret salt. Diagnostic and authentication logs are strictly sanitized to redact photos and tokens.

8. Children's Privacy

Plantii is an agricultural diagnostic tool designed for farmers and is not directed to children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update the version number and effective date. Significant changes will present the Consent Screen again when launching the app.
